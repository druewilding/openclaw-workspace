## What it looks like

Rugbrød is:
- **Dark brown** (almost blackish) on the outside
- Dense and hearty texture
- Packed with visible seeds (sunflower, flax, cracked rye)
- Typically baked in a loaves and sliced thick
- Used for smørrebrød (open-faced sandwiches)

The bread has a hard crust and a dense, seedy interior — very different from regular bread!
